## Chips reason to exist

I, Anna Gallone, am a programming/math/physics/board games/Litterature enjoyer. And Since I've had pretty good grades at university, I applied for and was received to a PhD contract at the Université Marie et Louis Pasteur in Besançon, France. My thesis is about the development and automatic control of dynamic distributed component-based systems.
The Chips language is the main tool that allows me to embody the concepts of my thesis. Therefore, while I am continuing to develop my thesis, I intend to only include to the language the features that are relevant for my studies. 
So, if anyone wants to contribute to the main branch of Chips, it would be under my close supervision (I guess), and only for adding features that I already plan to add. Other features CAN be developped, but won't be integrated to the main branch of the language for as long as I am a student.

Once my thesis is over, well... let's chat about what to add! I'd love to see how the language would evolve when put into the hands of other contributors. If you see any use that I haven't already thought of (and there must be a ton (or at least, that's what I wich for)), let's work on that together! Maybe someday, Chips will stand on its own two feets! That would be A DREAM! <3
