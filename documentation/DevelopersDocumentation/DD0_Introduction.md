# Developers Documentation

Hi fellow human! Anna here, I hope you like Chips so far! If you opened this document, it is either because you are lost, or because you are curious to know how to contribute to this project!
In the first case, There is nothing I can do for you. In the second case, you are in the right place! Enjoy reading.

Before diving into the details on how to contribute, I need to make the context of this language clear. This way, you can understand why the contribution process is described as it is.

## Chips reason to exist

I, Anna Gallone, am a math/physics/programming/board game/Litterature enjoyer. And Since I've had pretty good grades at university, I applied for and was received to a PhD contract at the Université Marie et Louis Pasteur in Besançon, France. My thesis is about the development and automatic control of dynamic distributed component-based systems.
The Chips language is the main tool that allows me to embody the concepts of my thesis. Therefore, while I am continuing to develop my thesis, I will only include to the language the features that are relevant for my studies. 
So, if anyone wants to contribute to the main branch of Chips, it would be under my close supervision, and only for adding features that I already plan to add. Other features CAN be developped, but won't be integrated to the main branch of the language for as long as I am a student.

Once my thesis is over, well... let's chat about what to add! I'd love to see how the language would evolve when put into the hands of other contributors. If you see any use that I haven't already thought of (and there must be a ton (or at least, that's what I wich for)), let's work on that together! Maybe someday, Chips will stand on its own two feets! That would be A DREAM! <3

## Contributing Guidelines

I will write here two sub-sections according to whether I am still doing my PhD thesis (which should end in october 2027, hopefuly) or not.

### Before October 2027

Anyone can pull and fork any branch of the repository, but I will only accept as contributors the people with who the scientific interest of a contribution to the language has been validated with my supervisors and I. Until the time I write this, only students working with us during one of their Master 2 degree project or internship have been integrated to the Github contributors.

If you already developped your own fork of the repository and believe it could be useful to integrate your work to the official Chips language, get in touch with me!

### After October 2027

I have no concrete idea on how open source repositories are managed. But yeah, that's the idea. Let's do that. But for sure, this language will be OPEN SOURCE and COMMUNITY MANAGED.
